What it is for

Turn blank NFC cards into ready-to-use PulseTap products.

The encoder writes a unique PulseTap claim link or destination to each physical card. It keeps the source file, next card, tag UID, verification result and completed count together so a large batch stays accurate from first card to last.

Production workflow

From spreadsheet to programmed card.

Three clear stages keep the operator moving and make every result traceable.

01

Load the queue

Upload a CSV or paste links directly. The tool validates each row and shows the next card before writing begins.

02

Tap and encode

Connect the reader, start the operation and place cards one at a time. Fast mode supports high-volume runs.

03

Verify and resume

Confirm the written value, record the UID and export progress. A saved session can continue at the correct row later.

Before you begin

The browser handles the workflow. The local bridge talks to the reader.

For security, a public website cannot directly control most USB NFC readers. Keep the PulseTap bridge running on the same Windows computer while using the online encoder.
